About me

An Engineer's Mindset.A Marketer's Perspective.

I'm Sujeeth Natarajan, a Digital Marketing Analyst with experience across SEO, performance marketing, content strategy, website optimization, and marketing automation. My journey into digital marketing wasn't a direct one. It began with engineering.

CHAPTER 01

Where It Started

I pursued a Bachelor of Engineering in Automobile Engineering, which helped me develop a structured way of thinking, approaching problems, and understanding systems.

But as I explored different career paths, I became increasingly interested in the digital space: how people discover information, how brands communicate their value, and how businesses build an online presence.

That curiosity became the starting point of my transition into digital marketing.

CHAPTER 02

Discovering Digital Marketing

After completing digital marketing training, I began applying what I learned through practical work.

My early experience at Bookaprop Estates introduced me to SEO fundamentals, keyword research, website content, and organic search visibility. At Cutibless, I expanded into paid advertising, social media, and creative development.

Digital marketing is not a single activity. It is a combination of strategy, execution, analysis, and continuous improvement.

CHAPTER 03

Growing Through Experience

At Cytrusst, I expanded my work into B2B cybersecurity marketing, website revamp activities, domain migration, content development, technical SEO coordination, and HubSpot form integrations.

Working in cybersecurity also taught me the importance of communicating complex products in a clear and accessible way.

I'm now exploring AEO and GEO alongside traditional SEO, while continuing to build my understanding of analytics, marketing automation, and digital strategy.
